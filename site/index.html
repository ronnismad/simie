Specification choices and limitations

Spectral units and collection

The printed equation contains λ/(hc), while the prose calls Φ a photon flux. This implementation takes Φ as spectral irradiance and converts once. The prose discusses η = 0.83, which is absent from the printed weight. η is used only in the separate baseline-current diagnostic, never silently inserted into the descriptor.

Numerical prescription

The supplement’s exact grids are used: wavelengths 320–900 nm every 2 nm; diameters 50–300 nm every 2 nm. The lognormal average uses its stated trapezoid rule, without renormalising truncated tails. σ/d = 0 is evaluated as the monodisperse limit. Under-resolved distributions are explicitly flagged.

Nine entries, eight in S5

The main text defines nine design entries, whereas S5 specifies eight network inputs. The new forward network retains all nine main-table entries. Side is retained as context; no separate back-side optical law is invented. Doping is encoded as the stated factor 1 or 0.85.

Finite predictions are not validation

Positive, finite responses can still exceed a photon budget. The diagnostic estimates baseline current with η = 0.83 and compares each member’s current with the above-bandgap unity-collection ceiling on the paper’s spectral grid. This check does not change the equations or rerank candidates.

What uncertainty means

The five-member standard deviation uses the 1/B convention. It measures sensitivity to resampled calibration rows, conditional on the model and provisional errors. It does not bound shared model error. Mesoporous γ and δ are unidentified by the anchors; zero solver values are conventions.

Reported optima

The displayed optical equation gives a boundary maximum at 260 nm within Table 1, rather than the reported 184 nm. This app does not change the weight to reproduce an annotation. Numerical proposals that fail scientific checks remain visible with their failure status.

Fresh training and reproducibility

Loading training provenance…

The forward model has three 64-unit tanh hidden layers. The bounded inverse has two 64-unit tanh hidden layers and learns through the frozen forward network, with the stated dispersion penalty. Adam settings follow S5. Fresh inputs, bootstrap fits and initial weights are used; no prior model outputs or weights are loaded.

Holdout metrics measure surrogate agreement with this physical model, not agreement with experiment. The prescribed S2 grid can produce numerical artefacts at very narrow dispersion. The app reports actual metrics rather than substituting published scores.
